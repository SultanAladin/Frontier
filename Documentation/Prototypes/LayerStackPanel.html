Layer Stack — current C++ state, transcribed

This mirrors LayerStackValidation.exe as it draws today. Numbers come from LayerStackPanel.cpp:44-55, colours from ColorPaletteDescriptor.cpp, the pose from InitializeLayerStackSample. Edit this file to fix the layout, then the C++ follows.

What the C++ claims about the card

  • An open row + its expand are ONE card. The row is the head: it owns top/left/right and drops its bottom edge; the expand closes the bottom.
  • An open head stops recolouring on hover, so the head can't light alone and split the card.
  • The opacity pill is a drag (ew-resize), not a click.
  • The caret toggles the fold without touching focus; clicking the row body focuses and opens.

Kind tints (LayerKinds.js)

  • Paint — hand-painted, accepts strokes
  • Fill — uniform authored values
  • Material — a PBR preset, flooded
  • Generator — procedural over the UV atlas

Things worth questioning

  • The rail has no scroll. Twelve open layers overrun the pane; the C++ just keeps drawing.
  • There is no drag-to-reorder on screen. RelocateLayer exists in LayerRecordTable.h but nothing calls it.
  • The head's tally is a bare count (6) while the foot says 6 / 12 — two different readings of the same fact.
  • Rename is stubbed: RenameOrdinal is seeded to -1 and never set.
  • The fold is instant — no micro-animation, unlike the card fold in ControlsGallery (FoldRate 0.28).
