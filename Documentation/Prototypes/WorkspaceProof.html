1 · Material workspace

Three-column authoring surface: material library on the left (127 presets, filtered by family), a live shaded PBR viewport in the centre, and a material inspector on the right whose controls are specific to the material's type — this one is layered automotive paint, so it exposes paint colour, roughness, depth, clearcoat gloss and a separate metallic-flake section.

3 · Pattern workspace

The 2D vector authoring canvas: motif layer list on the left, a ruled vector canvas with a tool bar and eight-handle selection in the centre, a seamless repeat preview beneath it, and the inspector on the right (align, surface channels, mask, transform). The workspace switcher across the top is Material · Pattern · Texture · Stamp · Baking.
