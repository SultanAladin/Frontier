Height fog. σt(h) = σ₀ · exp(−(h − hfloor)/H), uniform below the floor. Over a segment the optical depth is the antiderivative difference σ₀ · L · [F(h₁) − F(h₀)] / Δh with F(h) = H (1 − e^(−(h−h_floor)/H)) — exact for the stratified layer, so the march resolution never biases it. Near-level segments use the midpoint value, where that difference loses precision.

Banks. Ellipsoids with a soft radial mask, pooling toward the crown, an fBm clump texture, and a bounded-age backward characteristic that drags the noise through the wind and the curl eddies. Lift (positive condensation) thickens the fog; the radial outflow around a bank core clears it — the same FlowCouplingScale gain the solver applies.

Turn the closed form off to see the Riemann sum the CPU gate compares against: within ~0.2 % of the analytic depth at 28 steps on a grazing chord, visibly banded at 8. The sun column uses the analytic height term plus a tap march through the banks.
